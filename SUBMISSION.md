Title: Harvest Ready

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable crop-lot observation ledger with bounded AI advice. A grower freezes visible or measured readiness signs, records up to three observations, and GenLayer validators return a fixed-order mask plus READY, WATCH, or NOT_READY. The grower independently records HARVEST or WAIT; the AI cannot act. No sensors are queried, and the contract explicitly avoids food-safety, agronomy, weather, and regulatory claims.
